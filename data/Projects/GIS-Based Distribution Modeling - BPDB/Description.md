As a Junior Engineer on BPDB's GIS Based Distribution Network System project, I convert mapped distribution networks into electrical models in ETAP. Each model starts from GIS data. I rebuild the feeder topology, conductor specifications, transformer ratings, line lengths and connected loads as a one-line diagram.

The result is a structured, simulation-ready foundation for load-flow analysis, voltage profile assessment, feeder loading evaluation and short-circuit studies.

## Feeder models

| Feeder | What the model contains |
|---|---|
| 11 kV Feeder A | 8 distribution transformers, 13 overhead line segments, 66 buses and 44 cable and load records. Transformer capacity of 1,600 kVA documented. |
| 11 kV Feeder B | 28 transformer records and 44 documented feeder segments. 3,075 kVA total recorded transformer rating. |
| 11 kV Feeder C | 6 transformer records, 8 overhead line segments, 65 buses and 50 cable and load records in the model-input workbook. |

## Please note

The figures describe what each model contains. They are not simulation results. Feeder names are withheld because the underlying data belongs to BPDB.
