We built an 8052 microcontroller system that measures the frequency and duty cycle of a signal, compares two input frequencies, and generates an output signal at their sum.

The work covered 8051 assembly programming, Proteus simulation, an Arduino Mega, LCD interfacing and hardware testing with a logic analyzer.

## Result

In testing, a 10 kHz input was measured as 10,010 Hz, a measurement error of about 0.1%.
