We studied a regenerative braking system for electric vehicles in MATLAB. A DC shunt motor works as a generator during braking to recover kinetic energy as electrical energy, and a buck converter controls the recovered energy on its way to storage.

A DC shunt motor was chosen over an induction motor for its better speed control at partial loads.

## Scope

This was a design study. The report documents the system architecture and the energy conversion mechanism, but not simulated energy recovery figures.
