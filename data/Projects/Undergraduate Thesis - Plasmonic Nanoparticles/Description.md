I investigated how plasmonic coupling and thermal accumulation influence heat generation in randomly distributed silver nanoparticles under light illumination.

We used the discrete dipole approximation (DDA) to model the electromagnetic interactions between particles, and the thermal Green's function to calculate the spatial temperature distribution. The model places 1,000 silver nanoparticles at random inside a spherical region of water, lit by x-polarized light.

## Main finding

Plasmonic coupling can strongly increase heating at wavelengths away from an isolated nanoparticle's resonance, while reducing the temperature rise at the resonance wavelength.

## What I learned

The work built my foundation in computational electromagnetics, mathematical modeling and numerical analysis.
